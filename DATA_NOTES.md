# Data attribution and redistribution

The first release analyzes the redacted public Collusion.wiki export, published
by Sydney Von Arx, Cormac Slade Byrd, Spencer Kitts and Thomas Larsen.

- Findings: https://collusion.wiki/
- Export and provenance: https://collusion.wiki/explorer/download
- Source records: citations in each normalized event and evidence edge.

The public findings and download pages did not state an explicit general
dataset license when checked on October 3, 2026. RelayTrace's MIT license does
not apply to these data. The release includes a small, attributed evidence
snapshot supporting its demonstration. The original bulk export is excluded
from the release archive; use the acquisition command to obtain it from its
publisher. Avoid redistributing additional records without checking their
terms and redaction status.

The publisher has redacted personal information. This is not a guarantee that
all sensitive strings are absent. RelayTrace never uses credentials, executes
quoted code, or follows arbitrary URLs contained inside records.

AI Village is a separate, optional extension dataset:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/aidigestorg/ai-village. Access is reviewed by
its publisher. Its research terms prohibit training or fine-tuning without
written permission, require attribution, and prohibit re-identification.
No gated AI Village data is included in this release.

An agent's self-report is evidence of what it wrote, not independent proof of
what it read or did. Deleted, missing or inaccessible content is unobserved;
it must not be counted as evidence of failure.
